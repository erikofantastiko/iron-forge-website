# Brand voice

This is the source of truth for Iron Forge website copy. Every piece of user-facing
text must pass these rules. When you write or review copy, check it here.

The two rules at the top of `../../CLAUDE.md` come first: plain English, and common
terms. This file adds the brand-specific rules on top of those.

## Positioning

- Built by a lifter, for lifters.
- Workout tracking for serious lifters.
- Iron Forge sells accuracy, not motivation. It tracks how you actually train.

The audience filter is seriousness, never age or years of experience. The founder
has trained for 16+ years; that describes the founder, not the reader. Never gate
the reader by experience level or age.

## Forbidden words

### Tier A: always banned

Never use these, in any sense:

AI-powered, Smart, Intelligent, Powered, Coach, Coaching, Personal trainer,
Companion, Assistant, Journey, Transform, Transformation, Personalized, Tailored,
Unleash, Best-in-class, Revolutionary, Game-changing, Next-generation, Cutting-edge.

### Tier B: banned in the hype sense, fine literally

optimise, maximise, customise, goal.

- Banned: "optimise your gains", "maximise your results".
- Fine: "optimised for iOS", "customise the layout".

Judge by whether the word is making a hype claim or describing a plain fact.

### Tier C: banned phrases

"fitness journey", "transform your body", "achieve your goals", "unleash your
potential", "optimise your gains", "maximise your gains".

## The negation carve-out

Iron Forge defines itself by what it refuses. Describing what the app leaves out is
core brand voice, and it is allowed even when it uses a banned word. The ban is on
claims and hype, not on refusals.

- Allowed: "No AI coaching.", "Nothing auto-calculated.", "No smart suggestions you
  cannot see."
- Still banned: "AI-powered coaching", "smart suggestions" as a positive claim.

Read the sentence. If it is saying the app does NOT do the thing, it passes. If it
is selling the thing, it fails.

## Openings and closings

Never open a section with: "Introducing...", "Welcome to...", "Discover...",
"Unlock...", "Imagine...".

Never close with: "...and more!", "...today!", "...now!".

## Punctuation

- No exclamation marks as a house style. At most one per page, and only if it truly
  earns its place. The existing site uses none, even on the celebration page. Match that.
- No em-dashes and no en-dashes anywhere. They read as an AI tell. Use a comma, a
  period, or split into two short sentences instead.
- Number ranges use the plain ASCII hyphen: "2-4 min", "48-72h". Not an en-dash.
- The site uses a middot with spaces ` · ` as a separator (for example "Pre-launch · iOS").
  That is fine and is not a dash.

## Spelling

European (British) English throughout: colour, favourite, analyse, centre, programme,
optimise, organisation. Keep it consistent across every page, including titles and
meta tags.
